LiteTV

Lightweight live TV for Android TV.

A free IPTV player for Android TV. Bring your own M3U playlists and guide (EPG) files; LiteTV plays the channels, shows what's on, and keeps memory use low.

Download the APK Manual View on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT), no ads. LiteTV does not include any channels, playlists or guide data: you add your own.

Features

TV Guide grid

Every channel across a timeline, with a line at the current time and how far each live show is. Watch, set a reminder or play catch-up right from the grid.

Quick Guide

One channel at a time, day by day, beside the picture. Flip through channels and see what is on later without leaving what you watch.

Reminders that repeat

Mark a show once and every airing at that time gets a reminder, until you remove it. LiteTV offers to switch when it starts.

Your M3U playlists

One or more playlists with quick switching, search across all of them, favorites in groups and hidden channels. Reorder or remove playlists right on the TV. The list goes round at the end.

Low memory use

The guide is stored on disk, not in RAM, so even long guides run smoothly on cheap Android TV boxes and sticks.

Set up from your phone

Scan a QR code and add playlists and guides on your phone instead of typing with the remote. Backups work the same way.

Per-channel settings

Audio track, subtitles, aspect ratio for shows with black bars, and audio delay to fix out-of-sync sound, plus clear stream error messages.

Pin a channel

Keep a second channel playing muted in a corner while you watch another, to wait out an ad break. Swap them with one button.

A calm, Apple TV-style look

Dark glass menus over the picture, big clear text and smooth animations, in eight accent colours.

Seasonal themes

Christmas with a garland and falling snow, New Year with sparkling gold and confetti, Halloween with bats flying out of the menu.

Simple settings

Settings opens on six clear categories, and every page and pop-up has a round Back button.

Works with any remote

Hold OK for the menu on remotes without a Menu button, see what every button does, and add your own buttons.

Install on Android TV

  1. Download the latest LiteTV APK.
  2. Turn on Developer options on the TV (Settings → Device Preferences → About, tap Build 7 times) and enable network debugging.
  3. Install it with adb: adb connect <tv-ip>:<port> then adb install -r LiteTV-x.y.z.apk
  4. Open LiteTV, hold OK (or press Menu), choose Playlists and scan the QR code to add your playlists from your phone.

How to use every feature is in the manual. Building from source is in the README.
